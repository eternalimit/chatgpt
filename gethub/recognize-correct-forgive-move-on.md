# Recognize → Correct → Forgive → Let Go → Move On

## Sequence

RECOGNIZE → CORRECT → FORGIVE → LET GO → MOVE ON

## Definitions

- **Recognize:** acknowledge what occurred or what was found.
- **Correct:** amend an identified error without silently rewriting the record.
- **Forgive:** release resentment or continued punishment; forgiveness does not require erasure.
- **Let go:** stop holding onto the burden while allowing the record to remain.
- **Move on:** proceed forward after the matter has been recognized and addressed.

## Preservation rule

**MOVE ON ≠ ERASE**

The record remains. The process advances.
