# TCGE — Action of the Hydrogen Atom

## Formal distinction

STILL = defined quantum state at t₀.

ACTION = physical or state change described over time.

For hydrogen, action can include time evolution of the quantum state and observable processes such as absorption, emission, scattering, or response to an external field.

## Ground-state representation

ψ(r,t) = ψ(r) exp(-iEt/ℏ)

For a stationary state, the probability density |ψ(r,t)|² is time-independent while the quantum phase evolves.

## Energy transition

ΔE = hν

For hydrogen:
E_n = -13.6 eV / n²

A transition between levels can therefore produce absorption or emission of a photon.

## TCGE sequence

H → STATE → INTERACTION → CHANGE → ACTION

## Evidence boundary

This record formalizes the concept. It does not claim that every hydrogen-atom state exhibits observable motion in a classical orbital sense.
