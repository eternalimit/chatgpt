# Universe House Algorithm

## Purpose

Translate a house-acquisition vision into a defined, verifiable procedure.

## Procedure

VISION → REQUIREMENTS → SEARCH → FILTER → EVALUATE → VERIFY → PROCURE → CONFIRM → OUTPUT

### 1. DEFINE
Convert the vision into measurable requirements: location, budget, size, purpose, and ownership requirements.

### 2. SEARCH
Identify candidate properties or locations.

### 3. FILTER
Remove candidates that fail mandatory requirements.

### 4. EVALUATE
Compare price, financing, condition, location, legal status, and ongoing costs.

### 5. VERIFY
Independently verify title, inspection, financing, zoning, taxes, and other required records.

### 6. PROCURE
Make an offer or complete the applicable acquisition process.

### 7. CONFIRM
Verify that the transaction actually completed.

### 8. OUTPUT
HOUSE = VERIFIED ACQUIRED ASSET

## TCGE/TXGE mapping

VISION → TXGE → ACTION PLAN → TCGE GATE → VERIFIED RESULT

## Boundary

This algorithm is a planning specification. A record of the algorithm is not evidence that a property has been located, purchased, or transferred.
