# Formalizations — U

## U

`U` is preserved as a symbolic token. Its meaning is not assumed without a defined specification.

## Formalization

A formalization converts an informal concept into explicitly defined symbols, rules, states, or operations.

\[
\boxed{\text{CONCEPT}\rightarrow\text{DEFINITION}\rightarrow\text{RULE}\rightarrow\text{FORMAL MODEL}}
\]

## Boundary

An undefined symbol remains undefined until its meaning and permitted operations are specified.
