# Dictionary

This layer separates ordinary-language meanings from framework-specific glossary meanings.

## library
An organized collection of materials, information, or reusable resources.

## code
A system of symbols or instructions interpreted according to rules.

## unit
A single thing, quantity, or standard used as one element of a larger set or measurement.

## universe
A total domain under consideration; in ordinary scientific usage, the physical universe means all space, time, matter, and energy considered together.

## universal
Applying throughout or to all members of the stated class or domain.

## result
An outcome produced by an operation, process, or event.
