# Richard ATH Record

## Definition

ATH_R = Authenticated Truth/History Record for Richard

ATH_R = (S, I, T, P, C, V)

where:

- S = source
- I = stated intent
- T = timestamp / ordering evidence
- P = provenance
- C = cryptographic commitment
- V = verification state

## Governing sequence

SOURCE → RECORD → COMMIT → VERIFY → PRESERVE

## Verification rule

Each claim within ATH_R carries its own verification state.

The existence of the ATH record does not automatically promote every contained claim to VERIFIED.

## Boundary

ATH_R ≠ automatic proof of truth
ATH_R ≠ automatic proof of identity
ATH_R ≠ automatic proof of legal ownership
ATH_R ≠ automatic proof of authorship

ATH_R is a provenance/history construct for Richard.

A cryptographic commitment, blockchain anchor, identity assertion, authorship claim, or ownership claim requires its own defined mechanism and evidence before that property is classified as verified.
