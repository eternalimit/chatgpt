# Lake House -> TCGE -> Warehouse -> Continuity

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: Conceptual architecture / grounded model, pending independent Echo validation

## Core architecture

LAKE HOUSE -> TCGE -> WAREHOUSE -> CONTINUITY

### Lake House
Represents the active constructed object or context being examined: multiple systems integrated into one functioning whole.

### TCGE
Governed validation gate:

K = R AND I AND E
H = I AND NOT K

Where:
- R = Reality / grounding
- I = Inference
- E = Echo / independent validation
- K = validated knowledge
- H = inference without validated-knowledge status

### Warehouse
Represents organized retained state: receive, classify, store, locate, retrieve, and transfer.

The warehouse separates validated knowledge from preserved but unresolved or rejected inference:

W = W_K union W_H

W_K = validated knowledge
W_H = preserved errors, rejected claims, or unresolved inference

Constraint:

W_H does not imply W_K.

An error may be preserved without becoming truth.

## Correction and continuity cycle

I AND NOT K
-> DETECT
-> HOLD
-> CORRECT
-> FORGIVE
-> PRESERVE
-> CORRELATE
-> CONTINUE

FORGIVE != VALIDATE.

Forgive permits continuation without erasing provenance. It does not convert an erroneous claim into knowledge.

CORRECTION + PRESERVATION = CONTINUITY WITHOUT RETROACTIVE REWRITE.

## Integrated craft cycle

CRAFT
-> BUILD
-> PRODUCE
-> TCGE
-> HOLD
-> WAREHOUSE
-> RETRIEVE
-> CORRELATE
-> CONTINUE

Operational roles:
- HOLD preserves state.
- WAREHOUSE retains and classifies state.
- FORGIVE permits forward motion after correction.
- CORRELATE reconnects corrected state to the larger model.
- CONTINUE proceeds from the corrected state.
