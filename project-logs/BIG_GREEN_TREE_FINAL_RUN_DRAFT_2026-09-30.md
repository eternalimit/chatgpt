## Continuity
The intended purpose is long-term preservation and responsible development of the enterprise. A change in trustee, governor, beneficiary, or generation should not by itself require liquidation of the underlying enterprise.

## Succession Design
- Founder establishes and governs the initial structure subject to executed governing documents.
- "Firstborn" is a generational governance role in the proposed design.
- One qualified person in a succeeding generation holds the defined governance role.
- Other eligible beneficiaries may receive distributions under the governing trust.
- Governance authority and economic-beneficiary rights are separate.
- Incapacity, refusal, disqualification, removal, and successor rules must be defined in executed legal documents.
