## Definition

An Anchored Tiny Home (ATH) is a compact dwelling whose identity, location, physical interfaces, and evidence are bound to a defined POINT so the home can be governed from design through construction and verification.

ATH = (H, P, A, U, Ev, G)

Where:
- H = Home, the physical dwelling
- P = Point, the defined site/reference location
- A = Anchor, the physical connection between home and site
- U = Utilities/interfaces, such as water, sewer, power, and communications
- Ev = Evidence, the records describing or demonstrating the installed state
- G = Governance, the rules controlling permitted transitions

Governing relationship:

H --A--> P

H != P

The anchor establishes a relationship, not identity.
