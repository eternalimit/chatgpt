## Mediation rule
When the user's instruction/evidence and the model's interpretation conflict:
1. Preserve the original user position.
2. Preserve the verification finding separately.
3. Identify the exact disagreement.
4. Submit the evidence for independent review when available and authorized.
5. Do not convert either position into the other's position.
