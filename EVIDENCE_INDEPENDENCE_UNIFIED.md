# Evidence — Independence — Alone — Together — Unified

## Definitions

ALONE = single-source evidence.

TOGETHER = multiple observations considered jointly.

INDEPENDENCE = separate source or method.

UNIFIED = combined record that preserves source distinctions and provenance.

## Formal relation

E_unified = {E1, E2, ...}

Independence is not assumed merely because there are multiple records.

E1 ⟂ E2

means the evidence sources or methods are independent under the declared criterion.
