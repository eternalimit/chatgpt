# Manufacture — Universal to Simplified

## Universal

**Manufacture:** To transform a defined specification into a physical realization through a controlled process.

\[
\text{SPECIFICATION} \rightarrow \text{PROCESS} \rightarrow \text{PHYSICAL OBJECT}
\]

## Reduction

\[
\text{DEFINED STATE} \rightarrow \text{PROCESS} \rightarrow \text{REALIZED STATE}
\]

\[
\text{DEFINE} \rightarrow \text{TRANSFORM} \rightarrow \text{REALIZE}
\]

\[
\text{INPUT} \rightarrow \text{PROCESS} \rightarrow \text{OUTPUT}
\]

## Simplest

\[
\boxed{\text{MANUFACTURE}=\text{MAKE}}
\]

## Experimental distinction

Manufacture creates the testable physical instance. It does not establish that the specification or hypothesis is correct.

## Authorization / commitment distinction

\[
\text{AUTHORIZE} \neq \text{COMMIT} \neq \text{EXECUTE}
\]

- **AUTHORIZE:** permit an action or transition.
- **COMMIT:** make a state fixed and subsequently verifiable.
- **EXECUTE:** perform the authorized operation.
