# Forgive — Universal to Simplified

## Universal

**Forgive:** To release a held grievance or claim of resentment toward an acknowledged wrong.

## Reduction

\[
\text{WRONG} \rightarrow \text{ACKNOWLEDGE} \rightarrow \text{RELEASE}
\]

## Simplification

\[
\text{HOLD} \rightarrow \text{LET GO}
\]

## Simplest

\[
\boxed{\text{FORGIVE}=\text{RELEASE}}
\]

## Distinction

\[
\text{FORGIVE} \neq \text{FORGET} \neq \text{EXCUSE}
\]

Forgiveness can release the grievance without changing the historical record.
