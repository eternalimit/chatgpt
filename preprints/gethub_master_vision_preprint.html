Abstract

This preprint proposes a research architecture connecting residential water-service design, a candidate centralized distribution hub (“GETHUB”), a three-bit junction representation, physical manifold implementations, field measurement, and TCGE evidence governance. The research question is whether a centralized, observable and optionally controllable branch-distribution architecture can preserve acceptable hydraulic service while improving isolation, measurement, reproducibility, and evidence traceability.

1. Master Vision

Physical: street → meter → service → candidate GETHUB manifold → branches → fixtures.
Evidence: authoritative source / drawing / measurement → identity + provenance → GETHUB evidence transfer → TCGE/GATE → implementation → measurement → independent verification → append-only return.

GETHUB has two distinct research meanings: a governed evidence-transfer concept and a candidate physical centralized distribution hub. The latter remains a hypothesis requiring engineering validation.

2. Candidate State Representation

The design uses N = 4b₂ + 2b₁ + b₀ and proposes P = N + 1 as an encoding from eight binary states to one through eight connected paths. This is a design encoding, not a discovered law. Geometry Γ remains independent: equal port counts can have different geometries.

6. Experimental Program

Fabricate a defined manifold; document dimensions/materials; calibrate pressure and flow measurement; freeze inlet and branch conditions; test authorized configurations; compare observations against a preregistered model; repeat independently; retain raw data unchanged with cryptographic identity. Residential deployment would require separate code, material/listing, installation, pressure, backflow, accessibility, jurisdictional and permitting review.

7. Falsifiers

Revise or reject the physical hypothesis if the hub causes unacceptable service, cannot reproducibly select defined states, produces unexplained residuals beyond uncertainty, cannot satisfy applicable requirements, or provides no material advantage over a conventional manifold for the defined objective.
