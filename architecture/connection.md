# Connection

A connection is a preserved relation between two states or objects.

\[
\boxed{C:A\rightarrow B}
\]

Within the continuity model:

\[
\boxed{S_{t-1}\xrightarrow{C_t}S_t\xrightarrow{C_{t+1}}S_{t+1}}
\]

The connection itself does not imply direction, causality, ownership, or meaning beyond what is explicitly defined and verified.

\[
\boxed{\text{CONNECTION}=\text{PRESERVED RELATION}}
\]
