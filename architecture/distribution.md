# Distribution

\[
\boxed{\text{DISTRIBUTION}=\text{SOURCE}\rightarrow\text{DESTINATION}}
\]

Distribution assigns or routes an item from a defined source to one or more defined destinations.

Formalization:

\[
D(x,\{d_1,d_2,\ldots,d_n\})
\]

where \(x\) is the distributed item and \(d_i\) are destinations.

Operational checks:

\[
\boxed{\text{WHO/WHAT}\rightarrow\text{WHERE}\rightarrow\text{WHY}}
\]

Boundary:

\[
\boxed{\text{DISTRIBUTION}\neq\text{EXECUTION}}
\]

Distribution routes or assigns; execution performs the action.
