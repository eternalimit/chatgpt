# Perseverance → Peace → Fullness → Gratitude

A human-values sequence:

\[
\boxed{
\text{PERSEVERANCE}
\rightarrow
\text{PEACE}
\rightarrow
\text{FULLNESS}
\rightarrow
\text{GRATITUDE}
}
\]

- Perseverance = continuing despite difficulty.
- Peace = a state of calm or absence of conflict.
- Fullness = a sense of completeness or sufficiency.
- Gratitude = recognition and appreciation of what is valued.

Reduced:

\[
\boxed{\text{CONTINUE}\rightarrow\text{CALM}\rightarrow\text{ENOUGH}\rightarrow\text{APPRECIATE}}
\]

These are human states or values, not mathematical proof conditions.

\[
\boxed{\text{FEELING}\neq\text{FACT}}
\]
