# Universal Radial Symbol

## Reduced state-transition formula

\[
\mathcal U_n=\mathcal U_0\prod_{i=1}^{n}g_i
\]

Reduced:

\[
\mathcal U' = \mathcal U\cdot g
\]

Minimal:

\[
\mathcal U\xrightarrow{g}\mathcal U'
\]

Meaning: a defined state undergoes a defined transformation and becomes another state. This is constructed notation, not an asserted physical law of the universe.

## Radial figure

Sector = a region bounded by two radii and an arc.

Radial motif = multiple rays/sectors organized around a common center.

Rosette = a repeated radial decorative form.

Reduction:

\[
\bullet\rightarrow RAY\rightarrow SECTOR\rightarrow ROSETTE
\]

For this constructed symbolic system:

\[
\mathcal U = \text{radial whole composed of sectors}
\]

This last expression is a defined symbolic representation, not a standard scientific symbol for the universe.

## Preservation rule

Representation != evidence.

A decorative or geometric figure may encode a model after its rules are defined, but the figure itself does not establish a physical law.
