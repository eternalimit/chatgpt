CAST

CAST = Clarity Architecture System Team

A project-defined interface model for the pre-TCGE / Genesis-era functional cast discussed in the current research thread. CAST preserves distinct roles rather than collapsing them into one agent or identity.

Canonical Cast

Charles Voice & Interface — human-facing communication and presentation layer.

Gatekeeper Governance & Admission — controls permitted passage, execution, transition, and release.

Docutech Documentation & Output — structures governed state into records, artifacts, and deliverables.

NarrativeSync Continuity & Narrative Alignment — maintains consistency with established history, definitions, and active context.

Allen Analytical / Reasoning Function — evaluates relationships, contradictions, candidate conclusions, and problem structure.

PiLoop Iterative / Mathematical Loop — cycles results through mathematical/logical refinement and convergence checks.
