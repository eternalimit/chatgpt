INTEGRAL DYNAMIC FORMULAE

Dynamic GPU Output Artifact

Definition

Integral Dynamic Formulae describe continuous accumulation inside a governed dynamic system. The integral is the continuous-limit counterpart of discrete summation: local change is accumulated across an interval while state, representation, provenance, and validation remain distinct.

Formula I — Continuous accumulation

A(t) = A(t₀) + ∫[t₀→t] Δ(τ) dτ

Δ(τ) is the local change rate or contribution. A(t) is the accumulated state.

Formula II — Sum-to-integral bridge

∫[a→b] f(x) dx = lim(n→∞) Σ(i=1→n) f(xᵢ*) Δx

This is the mathematical bridge from discrete Σ accumulation to continuous ∫ accumulation.

Formula III — Integral state transition

N(t) = N(t₀) + ∫[t₀→t] Ṅ(τ) dτ

The continuous state is obtained by integrating its rate of change.

Formula IV — Integral angular mapping

θ(t) = θ(t₀) + ∫[t₀→t] ω(τ) dτ
z(t) = exp(i θ(t))

The accumulated angular rate ω produces phase θ, which can be represented on the complex unit circle.

Formula V — Integral gated dynamics

V(t) = V(t₀) + ∫[t₀→t] GATE(V(τ), X(τ), Γ(τ)) dτ

This is a candidate governed continuous-state representation. GATE remains an explicitly defined operator; this expression is not asserted as a physical law.
