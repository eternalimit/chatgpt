# Charles Verification — OBSERVE Definition

## Proposed Definition

`OBSERVE(O) = record the detectable state of O as evidence`

**VERIFIED as a FIDELITY working definition.**

It preserves the distinction:

`Observation != Interpretation != Explanation`

## Refined Definition

For greater rigor:

`OBSERVE(O,t) = Record(Detect(O,t))`

where:

- `O` is the object, event, relation, change, or measurable phenomenon under observation.
- `t` identifies the observation time or temporal scope.
- `Detect(O,t)` represents what the observer or instrument actually detects.
- `Record(...)` preserves the detected result as evidence.

## Charles Boundary

An observation establishes the recorded detection.

It does not, by itself, establish:

- the interpretation of that observation;
- the causal mechanism producing it; or
- the truth of a broader hypothesis.
